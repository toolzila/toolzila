The basics of image size

  • Pixels are the width and height of a picture. More pixels means more detail and a bigger file.
  • KB or MB is the file size. Forms usually limit this, not pixels.
  • Quality is how much detail the file keeps when saved. Between 60 and 80 percent is usually enough for photos.

Choosing a format

Use JPG for photos, PNG for logos and screenshots that need sharp edges or transparency, and WebP for websites when you want smaller files.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Every image tool works inside your browser, so your pictures stay on your device.

Why is my photo blurry after resizing?

Usually the original was small, or the target size was very low. Start from the largest original and use the biggest size limit the form allows.

Which format is best for a government form?

Most forms ask for JPG or JPEG. Follow the rules on the form you are filling.
