Photo & Signature Resizer

Resize a photo or signature to an exact size in KB for exam forms, job applications and government portals.

How to use Photo & Signature Resizer

  1. Choose your photo or scanned signature.
  2. Tap the size you need, such as 20 KB or 50 KB.
  3. Type width and height only if the form needs exact pixels, then download.

Features

  • Target size in KB
  • Optional width and height
  • Private, works in your browser

Supported formats and limitations

The result is a JPG. If you fix both width and height, a very small target size may not be reachable and the tool will tell you.

Frequently asked questions

How does it reach the size I ask for?

It lowers the image quality step by step until the file fits your target size.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. It never leaves your device.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
