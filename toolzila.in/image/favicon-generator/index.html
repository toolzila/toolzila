Favicon Generator

Make a favicon for your website from your logo, image, text or emoji. Download favicon.ico, PNG sizes, the Apple touch icon and the HTML code in one ZIP.

How to use Favicon Generator

  1. Choose your logo or image, or switch to Text or emoji.
  2. Pick the shape, padding and colours and check the preview.
  3. Click Download all (ZIP), upload the files to your website and paste the code into the head of your pages.

Features

  • ICO plus 16, 32, 48, 180, 192 and 512 px PNG icons
  • Includes site.webmanifest and the HTML code
  • Runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded

Supported formats and limitations

Works with PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF and SVG images up to 10 MB. For the sharpest icons use a square image of at least 512 x 512 px. The ICO file holds 16, 32 and 48 px icons. Emoji look depends on the emoji font of your device.

Frequently asked questions

Which files do I get?

A ZIP with favicon.ico, favicon-16x16.png, favicon-32x32.png, favicon-48x48.png, apple-touch-icon.png, android-chrome-192x192.png, android-chrome-512x512.png, site.webmanifest and a file with the HTML code.

Where do I put the files?

Upload them to the main (root) folder of your website and paste the code into the head section of every page.

Is my logo uploaded?

No. The icons are made in your browser.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
