HEIC to JPG

Convert iPhone HEIC and HEIF photos to JPG so they open everywhere.

How to use HEIC to JPG

  1. Choose one or more HEIC photos.
  2. Wait while each photo is converted.
  3. Download the JPG files.

Features

  • Many photos at once
  • Quality 92 percent
  • Runs in your browser

Supported formats and limitations

Works with HEIC and HEIF photos. Large photos can take a few seconds each. The converter library loads from the internet the first time.

Frequently asked questions

Why do iPhone photos not open on my computer?

iPhones save photos as HEIC, which some computers and websites do not support. JPG works almost everywhere.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. They are converted in your browser.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
