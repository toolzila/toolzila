JPG to PNG

Convert JPG images to PNG online. You can also convert PNG and WebP images to JPG or WebP.

How to use JPG to PNG

  1. Choose your JPG images.
  2. Check that PNG is selected as the output format.
  3. Download the converted files.

Features

  • Many images at once
  • Also converts to JPG or WebP
  • Runs in your browser

Supported formats and limitations

Converting JPG to PNG does not add detail or transparency, and PNG files are often larger than JPG. Inputs can be JPG, PNG or WebP.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the PNG bigger than my JPG?

PNG keeps every pixel without lossy compression, so photos usually become larger.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. Change the Convert to option to JPG.
