Background Remover

Remove the background from a photo and download a transparent PNG. Works in your browser with no upload and no API.

How to use Background Remover

  1. Choose a photo with a clear subject, such as a person, product or animal.
  2. Wait a few seconds while the cutout is made. Adjust Cutout strength and Edge softness if the edges need work.
  3. Pick a transparent, white, black or custom colour background and download the PNG or JPG.

Features

  • Transparent PNG or a new background colour
  • Cutout strength and edge softness sliders
  • Photo stays on your device, no API

Supported formats and limitations

Works best on photos where the subject stands out (people, products, animals). Fine hair, glass and busy backgrounds can leave rough edges, so use the sliders to improve them. Photos larger than 2000 px are reduced to 2000 px on the longest side. The cutout engine and model (about 7 MB when compressed) load the first time you use the tool and are then kept by your browser. Powered by U2-Net (Apache 2.0) and ONNX Runtime Web (MIT).

Frequently asked questions

Is it as good as paid tools?

Not always. Paid services use much larger models. This free tool is private and works well on clear subjects, and the sliders help clean up the edges.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The cutout is made inside your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.

Why is the first photo slower?

The model and engine load the first time. After that, photos are processed faster.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
