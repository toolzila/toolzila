How to use Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Read the live counts below it.
  3. Use Clear to start again.

Features

  • Words, characters, sentences and paragraphs
  • Reading time estimate
  • Works with Hindi and English

Supported formats and limitations

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. Very large texts may feel slow on old phones.

How it works

Reading time = words / 200 minutes, rounded. Sentences are counted by . ! ? and the Hindi danda.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text saved?

No. Counting happens in your browser.

How is reading time worked out?

We divide the word count by 200, an average adult reading speed.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
