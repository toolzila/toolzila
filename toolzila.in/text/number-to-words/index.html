Number to Words

Write any rupee amount in words using the Indian lakh and crore system.

-

How to use Number to Words

  1. Type the amount.
  2. Read the amount in words.
  3. Copy it for your cheque or invoice.

Features

  • Lakh and crore system
  • Paise supported
  • Copy in one tap

Supported formats and limitations

Works for amounts below 1,000 trillion with up to two decimal places. Always double-check important documents.

Frequently asked questions

Does it use lakh and crore?

Yes. It follows the Indian number system.

Can it write paise?

Yes. Add up to two decimals.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
