Case Converter

Change text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case in one tap.

How to use Case Converter

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Tap the case you want.
  3. Copy the result.

Features

  • Four case styles
  • Copy button
  • Works on any length of text

Supported formats and limitations

Case rules apply to English letters. Title Case capitalises every word and does not skip small words such as of or the.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo a change?

Tap another style, but the original capitals cannot be restored, so keep a copy of the original.

Is my text saved?

No. It stays in your browser.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
