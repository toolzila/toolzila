How to use Password Generator

  1. Choose the length and character types.
  2. A new password appears instantly.
  3. Click Copy and save it in a password manager.

Features

  • 8 to 64 characters
  • Uses your browser's secure random generator
  • Strength meter

Supported formats and limitations

Use a different password for every important account. Do not share passwords in chat.

Frequently asked questions

Is the password stored?

No. It exists only on your screen.

How long should it be?

At least 12 to 16 characters.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
