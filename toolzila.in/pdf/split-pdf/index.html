Split PDF

Keep only the pages you need from a PDF. Type page numbers like 1-3, 5 and download a new PDF.

How to use Split PDF

  1. Choose your PDF and wait for the page count.
  2. Type the pages to keep, for example 1-3, 5.
  3. Click Extract pages to download the new PDF.

Features

  • Page ranges and single pages
  • Original PDF is not changed
  • Runs in your browser

Supported formats and limitations

Only PDF files are accepted. Password-protected or damaged PDFs cannot be read. Page numbers outside the document are ignored.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pick pages that are not next to each other?

Yes. Separate them with commas, like 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
