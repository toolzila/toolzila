Merge PDF

Combine several PDF files into one document right in your browser. Reorder the files before you merge.

How to use Merge PDF

  1. Add two or more PDF files by tapping the box or dropping them in.
  2. Use the arrow buttons to put the files in the order you want.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and your combined file downloads.

Features

  • Reorder files before merging
  • Files stay on your device
  • No signup and no watermark

Supported formats and limitations

Only PDF files are accepted. Password-protected or damaged PDFs cannot be merged. Very large files depend on your device memory.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The merge happens inside your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Will the quality change?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so text and images stay the same.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
