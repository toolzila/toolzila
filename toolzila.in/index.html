    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Toolzila free?

    Yes. Every tool is free and you do not need an account.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. The tools run inside your browser, so your files and text stay on your device. Some tools download a helper library or language data from the internet the first time you use them.

    Does Image to Text need internet?

    Yes, the first time, to download language data. Your image itself is never uploaded.

    Which devices work?

    Any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer.
