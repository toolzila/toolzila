Text to Speech

Make your browser read text aloud. Choose a voice and speed.

How to use Text to Speech

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Choose a voice and speed.
  3. Click Speak. Click Stop to end.

Features

  • Voices from your device
  • Speed control
  • Works in your browser

Supported formats and limitations

Available voices depend on your device and browser. Hindi voices appear only if your device has them. Some browsers use an online voice service for certain voices.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the voice list different on my phone?

Voices come from your device and browser, so the list changes.

Can I download the audio?

No. This tool only plays the sound.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
