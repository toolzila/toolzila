Speech to Text

Speak into your microphone and see your words as text. Works in English and Hindi.

How to use Speech to Text

  1. Choose your language and click Start listening.
  2. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  3. Speak, then click Stop. Copy your text.

Features

  • English and Hindi
  • Live text while you speak
  • Copy button

Supported formats and limitations

Works best in Chrome and Edge. Your browser may send the audio to its own speech service to turn it into text, so avoid private information. A microphone and internet connection are needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is my voice saved by Toolzila?

No. Toolzila does not receive or store your voice. Your browser handles the speech recognition.

Why does it not work on my browser?

Some browsers do not support speech recognition. Try Chrome or Edge.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
