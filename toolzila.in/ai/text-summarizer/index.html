Text Summarizer

Shorten a long article or notes by picking its most important sentences. Works instantly in your browser.

How to use Text Summarizer

  1. Paste your article or notes.
  2. Choose how many sentences you want.
  3. Click Summarise and copy the result.

Features

  • 3, 5 or 7 sentences
  • Keeps the original order
  • Works offline in your browser

Supported formats and limitations

This tool selects the most important existing sentences. It does not rewrite them or understand meaning like an AI model, so it works best on clear articles. Input up to 50,000 characters.

How it works

Each sentence gets a score from how often its meaningful words appear in the whole text. The top-scoring sentences are shown in their original order.

Frequently asked questions

Is this AI?

No. It uses a simple word-frequency method, so it does not need an API and your text never leaves your browser.

Does it work in Hindi?

It can pick sentences from Hindi text, but results are best in English.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
