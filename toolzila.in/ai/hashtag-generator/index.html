Hashtag Generator

Turn your topic into a list of hashtag ideas for Instagram, YouTube and other social media.

How to use Hashtag Generator

  1. Type your topic or a few keywords.
  2. Choose how many hashtags you want.
  3. Click Generate and copy the list.

Features

  • Ideas built from your own words
  • 10, 20 or 30 hashtags
  • Copy all in one tap

Supported formats and limitations

Suggestions are built from your words with common endings. They do not use live trending data, so check popular hashtags on the platform before posting.

How many hashtags should you use?

Hashtags help people who do not follow you find your post. They work best when they are relevant to the picture or video, not when they are stuffed in by the dozen.

How to choose good hashtags

  • Mix a few broad tags (like #fitness) with several specific ones (like #homeworkoutforbeginners).
  • Add one or two tags unique to your brand or series so followers can find all your posts.
  • Use a local tag, such as your city, if your audience is nearby.
  • Avoid banned or misleading tags. Search a tag in the app first to see what kind of posts it shows.
  • Write multi-word tags in CamelCase, like #BiharFood, so they are easy to read and work better with screen readers. The tool creates lowercase tags, so capitalise them yourself before posting.

This tool builds ideas from your own words. It does not know live trends, so treat the list as a starting point and keep only the tags that truly match your post.
