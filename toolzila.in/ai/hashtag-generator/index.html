Hashtag Generator

Turn your topic into a list of hashtag ideas for Instagram, YouTube and other social media.

How to use Hashtag Generator

  1. Type your topic or a few keywords.
  2. Choose how many hashtags you want.
  3. Click Generate and copy the list.

Features

  • Ideas built from your own words
  • 10, 20 or 30 hashtags
  • Copy all in one tap

Supported formats and limitations

Suggestions are built from your words with common endings. They do not use live trending data, so check popular hashtags on the platform before posting.

Frequently asked questions

Are these trending hashtags?

No. They are ideas based on your words. Check the platform to see which tags are popular.

How many hashtags should I use?

Follow each platform's advice. A small set of relevant tags usually works best.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
