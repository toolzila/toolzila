Image to Text (OCR)

Extract text from a photo or screenshot in English or Hindi. Copy the text with one tap.

How to use Image to Text (OCR)

  1. Choose a clear photo or screenshot that contains text.
  2. Select the language in the image.
  3. Click Extract text, then copy the result.

Features

  • English, Hindi or both
  • Progress shown while reading
  • Your image stays in your browser

Supported formats and limitations

Works best on clear, straight, high-contrast printed text. Handwriting and blurry photos give poor results. Language data of a few MB downloads the first time you use it, so you need an internet connection. Always check the extracted text for mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. The text is read in your browser. Only the language data is downloaded.

Why are some words wrong?

OCR can misread small, blurry or stylised text. Use a larger, sharper image.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
