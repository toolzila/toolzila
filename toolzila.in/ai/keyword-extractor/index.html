Keyword Extractor

Find the most used words and phrases in any text, with counts and how much of the text they make up.

How to use Keyword Extractor

  1. Paste your article or text.
  2. Choose single words or two-word phrases.
  3. Click Find keywords to see counts and density.

Features

  • Single words or phrases
  • Count and density for each keyword
  • Ignores common filler words

Supported formats and limitations

Counts words that appear in your text. It does not know search volume or ranking. Needs at least 20 words. Filler words are removed for English only.

Frequently asked questions

What is keyword density?

It is how often a word appears compared with the total number of words, shown as a percentage.

Does it show search volume?

No. Use Google Search Console or Keyword Planner for that.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
