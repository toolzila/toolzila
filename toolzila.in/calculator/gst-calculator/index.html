How to use GST Calculator

  1. Enter the amount.
  2. Choose the GST rate and whether to add or remove GST.
  3. Read the GST amount, CGST, SGST and total.

Features

  • 5, 12, 18 and 28 percent
  • Add or remove GST
  • CGST and SGST split

Supported formats and limitations

Rates are the common slabs. Check the current rate for your product with the official GST rate list before filing or billing.

How it works

GST amount = price x rate / 100. Price without GST = total / (1 + rate / 100). Example: Rs 1,000 at 18 percent adds Rs 180, so the total is Rs 1,180.

Frequently asked questions

What are CGST and SGST?

For sales inside one state, GST is split equally into central (CGST) and state (SGST) parts.

Is IGST shown?

No. For sales between states the full GST is charged as IGST, which equals CGST plus SGST.
