Features

Supported formats and limitations

If the starting number for percent change is 0, the result cannot be calculated.

How it works

X percent of Y = X / 100 x Y. Percent change = (new - old) / old x 100. Example: 10 percent of 500 is 50, and going from 200 to 250 is a 25 percent increase.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a discount price?

Find the percent of the price, then subtract it from the price.

What does a negative result mean?

It means a decrease.
