How to use EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the yearly interest rate.
  3. Enter the tenure in years or months and read your EMI.

Features

  • Instant results while you type
  • Principal and interest split
  • Works for 0 percent loans

Supported formats and limitations

This is an estimate using the reducing-balance method. Banks may add fees or round the EMI slightly differently.

How it works

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (yearly rate / 12 / 100) and n is the number of months. Example: Rs 5,00,000 at 9 percent for 5 years gives an EMI of about Rs 10,379.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer tenure reduce the EMI?

Yes, the EMI becomes smaller, but you pay more total interest.

Does this include processing fees?

No. Add any bank fees separately.
