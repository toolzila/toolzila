How to use Age Calculator

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Leave Age on date as today, or pick another date.
  3. Read your age in years, months and days.

Features

  • Exact age in years, months and days
  • Total days lived
  • Days until next birthday

Supported formats and limitations

Dates use your device calendar. The Age on date cannot be earlier than the date of birth.

How it works

Age is the number of full years between the two dates, then the remaining full months and days. Example: born 15 Aug 2000, on 1 Nov 2026 the age is 26 years, 2 months and 17 days.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the Age on date field.

Is my date of birth saved?

No. It is only used inside your browser.

Privacy: this tool runs in your browser. Nothing you add is uploaded to our servers.
