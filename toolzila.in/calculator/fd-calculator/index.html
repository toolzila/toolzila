How to use FD Calculator

  1. Enter the deposit amount, yearly interest rate and time.
  2. Choose how often interest is added.
  3. Read the interest earned and maturity amount.

Features

  • Four compounding options
  • Deposit vs interest bar
  • Instant results

Supported formats and limitations

Banks may round or compound slightly differently, and tax (TDS) is not included. Use it as an estimate.

How it works

A = P x (1 + r/q)^(q x t), where P is the deposit, r the yearly rate, q the times interest is added per year and t the years. Example: Rs 1,00,000 at 7 percent for 5 years compounded quarterly gives about Rs 1,41,478.

Frequently asked questions

Which compounding should I pick?

Most banks use quarterly. Check your bank's terms.

Is TDS included?

No. Tax is not included.
